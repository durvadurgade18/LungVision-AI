FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Need Help?

Which lung diseases can LungVision AI detect?

LungVision AI is trained to detect seven major lung diseases including Tuberculosis, COVID-19, Lung Cancer, Pulmonary Fibrosis, Pulmonary Edema, Atelectasis, and Emphysema using chest X-ray images.

How accurate are the AI predictions?

The prediction accuracy depends on the quality of the uploaded chest X-ray. Our deep learning model has been trained on thousands of medical images to provide highly reliable results.

Are my uploaded X-ray images secure?

Yes. Your uploaded X-ray images are processed securely and are used only for disease prediction. We prioritize user privacy and data security.

How long does prediction take?

Prediction is usually completed within a few seconds after uploading the chest X-ray image.
