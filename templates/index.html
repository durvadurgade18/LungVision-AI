AI Powered Lung Disease Detection

Our deep learning model analyzes chest X-rays and detects multiple lung diseases with high accuracy.

Tuberculosis

A bacterial infection that mainly affects the lungs and spreads through the air.

COVID-19

Viral respiratory disease that can cause lung inflammation and breathing difficulty.

Lung Cancer

Abnormal growth of lung cells that can spread if not detected early.

Pulmonary Fibrosis

Scarring of lung tissue that reduces the lungs' ability to supply oxygen.

Pulmonary Edema

Fluid buildup inside the lungs that makes breathing difficult.

Emphysema

Damage to the air sacs of the lungs that reduces airflow and oxygen exchange.

Atelectasis

Partial or complete collapse of the lung causing reduced oxygen supply.
